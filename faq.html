FAQs

Do I need to know GIS?

No. Tell us about the location and what you want to understand. We select the analysis.

What locations can you analyse?

Locations where suitable geographic, satellite and environmental data are available. Coverage varies by location.

Do I need to provide a property boundary?

Not always. A location or coordinates may be enough for some products. A precise boundary helps for property- or land-specific analysis.

How long does a report take?

It depends on the product, location and depth of analysis. You'll get an estimated delivery time before you confirm the order.

Can you analyse a property I'm thinking of buying?

Yes, as geographic and environmental decision support. It doesn't replace legal title checks, valuation, structural inspection, engineering assessment or statutory approvals.

Can you tell me if land is suitable for development?

We provide spatial suitability screening and environmental analysis. It isn't planning permission, engineering certification or statutory approval.

Can you analyse groundwater?

We can do groundwater-related spatial screening using geographic, geological, terrain and rainfall data. Site-specific decisions may still need a professional hydrogeological investigation.

Can you monitor my farm?

Yes. Suitable agricultural locations can be monitored periodically with satellite imagery and environmental datasets.

Can you compare two properties?

Yes. We build comparative reports for multiple locations using relevant criteria.

Can I request something not listed?

Absolutely. Tell us what you're trying to understand and we'll tell you if ICC can help.
