How it works

  1. Tell us where

    Send a Google Maps location, coordinates or a site boundary.

  2. Tell us why

    Choose what you're trying to understand, or simply say "I don't know what I need. Just tell me what I should know."

  3. We analyse

    We use suitable geographic, satellite, environmental and spatial datasets: earth observation, GIS, remote sensing, climate data and spatial analysis.

  4. We make it simple

    Maps, imagery, charts, comparisons and plain explanations.

  5. You receive your report

    A clear digital report designed around your location and your question.
