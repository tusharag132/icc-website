More than a map

A map shows what is there. Analysis shows what has changed. ICC goes one step further and helps you understand what it means.

Data: vegetation index. Visual: vegetation map. Insight: "Vegetation is concentrated in the northern portion of the site and has stayed relatively stable over the analysed period."

That's the difference between data and intelligence.
