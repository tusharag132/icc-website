We make geographic information useful

Places are more than coordinates on a map. They have terrain, climate, water, vegetation, history, development and relationships with everything around them.

ICC combines geography, Earth observation, remote sensing, spatial analysis and environmental data to turn those layers into information people can use. Our goal is simple: make complex geographic intelligence accessible, visual and useful.

Who ICC is for

Individuals

Property buyers and owners, landowners, farm owners, travellers, creators.

Businesses

Hotels, resorts, hospitality, agriculture, retail and other location-dependent businesses.

Professionals

Architects, landscape designers, consultants, researchers, planners, developers.

Organisations

NGOs, universities, institutions, tourism and heritage organisations.

Behind every report

Depending on the project, we work with satellite imagery, GIS, remote sensing, Earth observation, terrain and elevation data, climate and environmental datasets, spatial modelling and data analysis. We choose the data and methods according to the question, not the other way around.
