What can we do?

Don't worry about the technical terms. We turn geographic data into things you can see and understand.

Where is it?

Maps, roads, settlements, nearby places, accessibility, surrounding land use.

What is the land like?

Elevation, slope, aspect, terrain and drainage patterns.

How green is it?

Vegetation, green cover, seasonal patterns and vegetation change.

What about water?

Water bodies, rainfall, drainage, watersheds and historical water change.

What is the climate like?

Temperature, rainfall, seasonality, heat patterns and trends.

What has changed?

Built-up growth, vegetation, land cover and water, compared across time.

What's around it?

Roads, settlements, facilities, attractions, natural features, heritage.

What stands out?

Instead of dozens of maps to interpret, we highlight the patterns that matter for your report.
